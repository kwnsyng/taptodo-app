TapToDo Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026 · 한국어

TapToDo (the "app") does not collect personal information. This policy applies to TapToDo 1.0 and later on iOS and Android.

1. Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other identifier, and it does not collect usage data.

2. Information stored on your device

Task titles, notes, categories, due dates, reminder times, and display settings are stored only on your device: in UserDefaults on iOS and in the app's private storage (DataStore) on Android. This data is never sent to the developer or to any server, and the developer cannot see it.

Due-date reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Their contents are not sent anywhere.

3. Android app

  • The Android app does not request the INTERNET permission.
  • Task data is excluded from Google cloud backup and device-to-device transfer, so it does not move to a new phone.
  • The only permissions the app requests are for reminders: showing notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS), exact-time alarms (SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM), and rescheduling reminders after the device restarts (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED).

4. Third-party services

The app contains no third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising, or payments.

5. Retention and deletion

All data stays on your device. Deleting a task, clearing the app's data, or uninstalling the app deletes that data. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request deletion of.

On iOS, if iCloud Backup is turned on, app data may be included in Apple's device backup, which is governed by Apple's policies.

6. Tracking and ads

TapToDo shows no ads and does not track you.

7. Payments

The current version is free and has no in-app purchases. If paid features are added, this policy will be updated first.

8. Children

The app does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
