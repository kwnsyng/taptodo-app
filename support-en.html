FAQ

Q. How do I delete my data?
All of your tasks are stored only on your device. Delete tasks in the app, or uninstall the app, and the data is gone.

Q. Will my tasks move to a new phone?
Version 1.0 stores tasks only on your device. On iOS, restoring a new device from an iCloud backup may bring them along. On Android, tasks are excluded from Google backup and device transfer, so they do not move to a new phone.

Q. I'm not getting reminders on Android.
Allow notifications in Settings > Apps > TapToDo. To get reminders at the exact time, tap "Open exact alarm settings" in the app's settings and turn the permission on.

Q. Which languages are supported?
English and Korean. The app follows your device language. On Android 13 and later you can also pick a language for TapToDo alone in Settings > Apps > TapToDo > Language.
